# Aelorien

**Type:** Aelorin Settlement (oldest, amphitheater formation)
**Kingdom/People:** Aelorin
**Position:** Central Greatwood — the Vale of First Song
**Story relevance:** Game One background lore; possible Game Two or Three location

---

## Physical Description

The oldest Aelorin settlement, predating the Greatwood's current extent. Built into a natural amphitheater formation — a shallow bowl in the terrain with unusually good acoustics. The Aelorin consider the shape itself sacred: the First Song was sung here and the land remembers it.

The Hall of the Vigil stands at the amphitheater's center — where Aelthiren who have begun the long transition rest in assisted stillness, tended by kin. It is not a hospice. It is more like a chrysalis.

Map note: Star symbol with amphitheater curve. Deep Greatwood position.

## Art Direction Notes

The oldest place in the Greatwood. The trees here predate the current extent of the forest — they may be even older and larger than those of Lirien-Thal. The bowl terrain creates a moment where Roland descends, which is directionally opposite to the Lirien-Thal canopy ascent.

The Hall of the Vigil at the center: where the transitioning Aelorin rest. The visual language should be stillness — no motion, no torch-flicker, natural light filtering from above. The Aelthiren in transition are not distressing to look at; they are peaceful. The horror (if any) is philosophical, not visual.

The First Song connection: this place has acoustic resonance. Consider whether environmental audio design (echoes in the bowl, faint harmonic tones) communicates this without exposition.
