# Karaz-Dûn

**Type:** Dwarven Hold
**Kingdom/People:** Northern dwarves (Dragon-Watcher clan leadership)
**Position:** Below Kara-Thûn, northern Spine of the World
**Story relevance:** Game One, Act III (Copper Disc arc; Dragon-Watcher records; Thrarin's treasury)

---

## Physical Description

The forges have not gone cold in four thousand years. The claim is made seriously and verified by the Dragon-Watchers, whose headquarters are here. The hold's upper levels are warm year-round from forge heat rising through the rock. The mountain above — Kara-Thûn — is the tallest in the Spine, permanently snow-crowned, visible from the Central Plains.

Karaz-Dûn's main exports: worked iron, precision instruments, the Underway maintenance contracts. The Dragon-Watchers' records of Drûn-Khazad's activity are housed in a fire-resistant vault at Level 3.

Map note: Mountain-gate icon below Kara-Thûn (tallest named peak).

## Art Direction Notes

Orange-amber dominant light throughout the upper levels from forge heat. The Dragon-Watcher records vault at Level 3 breaks this — cooler, more sober, fire-resistant stone (a deliberate visual transition: stepping into the vault is stepping away from the forge warmth).

Kara-Thûn above: tallest peak in the Spine, permanently snow-crowned. The contrast of the snow-white summit above and the forge-warm interior below is a spatial identity statement.

Bromrin appears briefly here in Game One — his mind is intact, but foreshadowing of Game Two deterioration is built into ambient behavior (a repeated phrase, a moment of unusual stillness). This is a behavioral note, not a geometry note.
