# Vosskar-on-the-Iron

**Type:** City
**Kingdom/People:** Vosskara
**Position:** Northern Ashfields, where the Vossrun river meets the Spine foothills
**Story relevance:** Game One, Act II (Bronze Ring arc; the listening mechanic lives here)

---

## Physical Description

Built in a river bend where the Vossrun curves, giving three natural sides of water defense. Iron-banded walls — the iron mined from the Spine's eastern face, hammered into bands along the stone wall courses as both reinforcement and statement. The city wastes nothing and decorates nothing. Buildings are garrison-functional: wide doors for equipment, low ceilings for heat retention, shared walls for structural efficiency.

Yaromir's citadel squats at the city's center — deliberately squat, deliberately ugly. Low profile for defensive purposes. The war council chamber is underground.

The ash-haze is visible from Vosskar's eastern wall on clear days. Goblin raids on outlying farmsteads are common enough that the city maintains a standing rotation of response cavalry.

Map note: Fortress-tower icon. River bend shown.

## Art Direction Notes

No warmth in the architecture — this is a city that considers decoration a military liability. Iron banding on the walls is the only distinctive visual element, and it is structural. The color palette is grey stone + iron grey + the distant ash-haze to the east.

The war council chamber underground is the most militarily austere room in the game. Torch-lit only. No decoration. Maps on a central table. The underground position is both practical (defensible, stable temperature) and symbolic — decisions made here are made below ground, away from politics.

Contrast with Solgrade: Vosskar is everything Solgrade refuses to be, and vice versa.
