# Brightwatch

**Type:** Fortress (Iron Chalice forward operating base and training facility)
**Kingdom/People:** Iron Chalice Order / Eldermark
**Position:** Eastern Eldermark frontier, where cultivated land ends at the Ashfields approach
**Story relevance:** Roland's training and expulsion site (backstory); Act IV Scene 1 — garrison camp on the Ashfields approach; Act IV Scene 3 — garrison fighters hold the rear during the fighting retreat; Game Three — Brightwatch garrison marches to Drûn-Khazad

---

## Physical Description

A walled fortress on a shallow rise at the last edge of productive Eldermark land. From the watchtower, you can see exactly where the green ends — a hard line where the soil thins and the ash-haze begins. Brightwatch was positioned here deliberately. The Order's doctrine is about holding ground, and this is the ground that needs holding.

Not large by city standards — the compound fits perhaps four hundred people at full garrison. Stone walls, two gated entries (main gate west toward Aldenholt, sally port east toward the Ashfields), three towers. The watchtower is the tallest thing visible for miles in every direction.

Inside: training yards that take up roughly a third of the compound. Barracks. A small infirmary. Stabling. A chapel — low-ceilinged, plain stone, one iron copy of the founding chalice. No decoration beyond that. The chapel is used more than most garrison chapels: the doctrine of endurance is devotional, and the people stationed at Brightwatch know better than most what they are enduring.

The garrison is mixed. Iron Chalice knights in varying numbers depending on the season's contract deployments. Eldermark levies doing frontier service — typically a year, typically assigned from the eastern towns. Trainees in their evaluation year. Some have iron-signed and are waiting for posting elsewhere. Others have been here long enough that Brightwatch has become the assignment rather than the waypoint.

Roland trained here for four years. He came back twice as an instructor. He was here when his expulsion order arrived. He left within the hour, on foot heading west. He has not been back.

## Art Direction Notes

The dominant color is grey stone, but not the cold grey of northern fortresses. Brightwatch stone is warm-grey — the local quarry, which runs slightly ochre. The ash-haze visible to the east bleaches the light differently than overcast sky: flatter, slightly wrong. The garrison learns to read it. It doesn't bother them the same way it bothers visitors.

Torches rather than lanterns on the walls at night — the Iron Chalice does not spend money where it does not have to. The forge on the east side of the compound runs most days. Weapon maintenance is constant.

The training yard in active use: noise, dust, the crack of practice blades. The same yard in the early morning before training: quiet, empty, long light coming in from the east over the Ashfields. This is where Roland liked to be. The memory is specific — it will surface at a meaningful moment if the writing gives it room.

Act IV approach: the garrison camp visible on the near side of the Ashfields is a forward observation post, roughly a half-day's ride east of the fortress itself. Lower profile than the fortress — tents, a fire, watch-positions. The fortress is implied in the distance behind it but not the scene location.
