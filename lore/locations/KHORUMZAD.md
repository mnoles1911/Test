# Khorumzad

**Type:** Dwarven Hold
**Kingdom/People:** Central dwarves
**Position:** Below Khorumzad's Crown, central Spine of the World
**Story relevance:** Game One background / future — Vault of Aen-Vael is here; Gold-hunger infection is the Game Two arc; Level 9 is the prison-repository of the Sundered Crown components (lore context only for Game One)

---

## Physical Description

Nine levels deep. The upper levels are prosperous and open to trade; the lower levels are increasingly restricted. Level 4 is where the gold-hunger infection begins its spread in Game Two — a contamination spreading through the rock itself, affecting those who work in contact with certain deep-vein deposits. Level 9 is where the Vault of Aen-Vael is accessible — the prison-repository of objects too dangerous to use and too significant to destroy.

The Crown above is broad and flat-topped, distinctive in profile from the other Spine peaks.

Map note: Mountain-gate icon (deepest arch symbol to indicate depth).

## Art Direction Notes

The progressive restriction of the nine levels is the defining spatial experience of Khorumzad. Upper levels: trade-bright, warm, open. Each level descending: corridor narrows, ceiling lowers, light grows colder and scarcer. Level 9 / Vault of Aen-Vael: maximum claustrophobia, the camera arm at its shortest, the elevation at its steepest.

The Vault of Aen-Vael contains objects too dangerous to use and too significant to destroy. The visual language should suggest preservation under extreme constraint — objects displayed or stored in locked cases, the space itself a kind of argument against access.

Gold-hunger infection (Game Two): the contamination spreading through rock at Level 4 will need a visual indicator when designed — perhaps a subtle discoloration in the stone, a yellow-gold stain spreading through the walls.
