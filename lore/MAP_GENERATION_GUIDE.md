# Map Generation Guide — Mira-Thal

This file is formatted as a reference prompt for generating a Tolkien-style hand-drawn map of the world of Mira-Thal. Feed this document to an image model alongside any style references.

---

## Style Directive

Draw a hand-illustrated fantasy world map in the style of J.R.R. Tolkien's maps from The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit, as illustrated by Christopher Tolkien after Tolkien's original sketches. Specific style elements:

- **Medium**: Ink on aged parchment or cream paper. Slight yellowing at edges. Faint water-stain texture acceptable.
- **Line weight**: Variable. Coastlines are the heaviest lines. Rivers medium weight. Roads thin and dashed. Mountain hatching fine.
- **Mountains**: Drawn as rows of side-view peaks with hatched shadows on one side. Not aerial-view — the classic illustrated profile-peak style. Named peaks slightly taller than their neighbors.
- **Forests**: Individual tree symbols (small conical or rounded crowns), clustered to suggest density. Not a filled silhouette — individual trees you can count at the edges.
- **Marshes**: Horizontal dash clusters with occasional dead-tree symbols.
- **Lettering**: Hand-lettered in a Tolkien-adjacent style. Region names in larger capitals, settlement names in smaller script, geographic feature names in italic.
- **Compass rose**: Present in one corner, simple eight-point design with N/S/E/W labeled.
- **Border**: Simple ruled border, slightly irregular as if hand-drawn with a ruler.
- **Sea**: Light horizontal line hatching in open water areas. Slightly denser near coastlines. Small illustrated ship in the Shroud Sea area.
- **Color palette** (if colored): Aged parchment background (#E8D5A3), forest green for tree symbols, blue-grey for mountains, dark blue for water lines, brown-red for roads, black for all linework and text.

---

## Map Layout

The map shows **two continents** on a single sheet, oriented with north at top.

- **Left two-thirds of the map**: Mira (western continent)
- **Right third of the map**: Thal (eastern continent)
- **Between them**: The Shroud Sea, shown with storm-hatching and a label reading "THE SHROUD SEA" in large capitals with a subtitle "Here storms do not end"
- **Below Mira's southern coast**: The Copper Isles (small island cluster)
- **Off Mira's western coast**: The Pilgrim's Chain (three small islands in a diagonal line)

---

## Mira — What to Draw

### Coastline (draw first, heaviest line weight)
- **West coast**: Dramatic, irregular cliff coastline. Suggest cliff faces with short perpendicular tick marks along the coast line. Two named bays:
  - **Bay of Mourne** (northwest): broad bay, deepest indentation in the western coast. Caer Brannoch sits on the northern headland.
  - **Bay of Sulwyn / Drowned Bay** (just south of Mourne): slightly smaller bay. Mark with a small sunken-ruin symbol — a tower partially submerged.
- **South coast**: Gentler, more rounded coastline. The Copper Shelf shown as a lighter-shaded shallow area just offshore.
- **East coast (Shroud Shore)**: Rough, minimal indentation. No harbors. Label: *"The Shroud Shore — no harbour here"*
- **North coast**: Rocky, irregular.

### Mountains — The Spine of the World
A continuous north-to-south mountain range through Mira's right third. Draw as a long chain of profile-peaks running roughly top-to-bottom of the map on the right side of Mira. Three peaks are taller and named:
- **Kara-Thûn** (northern Spine): tallest peak, snow-cap indicated with white at summit
- **Khorumzad's Crown** (central Spine): broad flat-topped profile, distinctive from the pointed peaks around it
- **The Broken Fang** (southern Spine): twin-peak silhouette, a visible split between the two summits

### Forests
- **The Greatwood**: Large forest covering the northern quarter of Mira. Dense tree symbols. Slightly darker/older-looking than normal forest. Label in center: *"THE GREATWOOD"*. Within it, mark Lirien-Thal with a star-and-tree symbol and small label.
- **The Weeping Wood**: Smaller patch of dead forest, northeast of center Mira, south of the Greatwood's eastern edge. Use bare-branch tree symbols (no canopy). Surround with a faint fixed-cloud symbol overhead.
- **Central Plains woodlots**: Scattered small clusters of trees across the central farmland area. Much sparser than the Greatwood.

### Rivers
- **The Aldwater**: The dominant river. Starts in the northern Spine (near Karaz-Dûn), flows west and southwest across the Central Plains, passes through Aldenholt, empties into the Bay of Mourne. Two-line river symbol for the lower navigable section.
- **The Silverthread**: Flows south from the Greatwood's southern edge, joins the Aldwater just east of Aldenholt. Single-line river.
- **The Vossrun**: Flows northeast from the southern Spine (near Kazaad-Brak), passes through Vosskaran territory, disperses into the Sorrowmarsh. Single-line river.
- **The Greyflow**: Flows southeast from the Sorrowmarsh to the Shroud Shore. Single-line river, label it in smaller italic: *"the Greyflow"*

### Special Terrain
- **The Ashfields**: East of the Spine's eastern face. Light grey stippling increasing toward the east. Label: *"THE ASHFIELDS"*. Small garrison-town symbols along a north-south road (the Frontier Road).
- **The Sorrowmarsh**: Southeast of the Spine's southern end, north of the Greyflow. Marsh symbols (horizontal dashes, dead trees). Label: *"SORROWMARSH"* with italic subtitle *"where nothing grows"*.

### Settlements on Mira
| Name | Symbol | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Aldenholt** | Walled city (multiple towers, crenellated) | At river confluence, center-left |
| **Caer Brannoch** | Coastal fortress (tower on cliff) | Northern Bay of Mourne headland |
| **Vosskar-on-the-Iron** | Fortress tower | Northern Ashfields, Spine junction |
| **Solgrade** | Open city (towers, no wall shown) | Southern Central Plains |
| **Lirien-Thal** | Tree with star above | Deep Greatwood, north-center |
| **Karaz-Dûn** | Mountain gate (arch in cliff face) | Below Kara-Thûn, northern Spine |
| **Khorumzad** | Mountain gate (deeper arch) | Below Khorumzad's Crown, central Spine |
| **Kazaad-Brak** | Mountain gate | Below the Broken Fang, southern Spine |
| **Coldstoke** | Small cross or hamlet symbol | Eastern Vosskaran frontier |

### Roads on Mira
- **The Salt Road**: Dashed line running north-south along the western coast
- **The King's Road**: Dashed line east-west from Aldenholt to the Spine
- **The Frontier Road**: Dashed line north-south along the Spine's eastern face through the Ashfields
- **The Underway**: Dotted line (underground) connecting the three dwarven holds beneath the Spine. Label: *"the Underway (below)"*

---

## The Shroud Sea — What to Draw

Occupies the center-right portion of the map between the two continents.

- Dense horizontal line hatching, slightly darker/more chaotic than the open western ocean, to suggest permanent storm
- Spiral or arc line symbols to indicate persistent wind/storm patterns
- One illustrated sailing ship, small, positioned in the western (calmer) edge of the sea with the label *"Brotherhood charts end here"*
- Label across the center of the sea in large italic capitals: *"THE SHROUD SEA"*
- Subtitle below in smaller text: *"no passage save the window"*
- In the northeastern section of the sea, faint ghost-ruins of the Aescryd (sunken continent) visible as dotted outlines beneath the hatching with label: *"Aescryd (drowned)"*

---

## Thal — What to Draw

### Coastline
- **West coast**: Rocky, minimal indentation, no bays. Short cliff-tick marks along the coastline.
- Smaller overall than Mira. More irregular outline.

### Mountains
- **The Vrothmor Peaks**: Central-left Thal. Cluster of jagged irregular peaks (more chaotic than Spine style — volcanic rather than glacial). Draw peaks as sharper, more angular. Add small steam-vent symbols (upward-curving lines) at several locations.
- **Drûn-Khazad**: Center of Thal, slightly south of the midpoint. Draw as a broad, wide mountain with a flattened summit and visible caldera depression. A glow symbol (small radiating lines or red-tinted area) in the caldera. Ash-cloud extending west-northwest from the summit as a dashed/stippled smear. Label prominently: *"DRÛN-KHAZAD"* with subtitle *"the Burning Wound"*.

### Other Features
- **The Ash-Steppe**: Western Thal between the coast and the Vrothmor Peaks. Open flat space with very light stippling. Scattered small stone-circle symbols for pre-First Age ruins. Label: *"THE ASH-STEPPE"*.
- **Mor-Vethrin**: On the eastern cliff-face of the Vrothmor Peaks. Dark fortress symbol, smaller than the human city symbols. Label in darker ink.
- **Naergrim territory**: Label the area around Mor-Vethrin and the eastern Vrothmor Peaks: *"Naergrim lands"* in smaller italic.

---

## Islands

**The Copper Isles** (south of Mira)
- Small cluster of 5–7 islands on the southern shelf
- Main island slightly larger than the others
- Small harbor symbol on the main island
- Label: *"THE COPPER ISLES"*

**The Pilgrim's Chain** (west of Mira)
- Three islands in a gentle northeast-to-southwest diagonal
- Each island has a small Aelorin-glade symbol (open circle with rays, like a simplified sun)
- Label: *"the Pilgrim's Chain"*

---

## Text and Labels Priority

In order of label size (largest to smallest):
1. THE SHROUD SEA (sea name)
2. MIRA / THAL (continent names, placed in open areas)
3. THE GREATWOOD, THE SPINE OF THE WORLD, THE ASHFIELDS, DRÛN-KHAZAD (major region/feature names)
4. Aldenholt, Caer Brannoch, Solgrade (major city names)
5. Bay of Mourne, Sorrowmarsh, Ash-Steppe, Vrothmor Peaks (secondary feature names)
6. River names, road names, smaller settlement names (smallest, italic)

## Suggested Image Prompt (condensed)

> A hand-drawn Tolkien-style fantasy world map on aged parchment showing two continents: Mira (left, larger, with dramatic western sea-cliffs, a north-south mountain spine, a vast northern forest, and farmland plains) and Thal (right, smaller, volcanic, dominated by a massive central shield volcano called Drûn-Khazad with a glowing caldera and permanent ash cloud). Between them, the Shroud Sea filled with storm-hatching. Profile-peak mountain illustration style. Individual tree symbols for forests. Hand-lettered labels. Rivers flowing west from the mountains across the plains. Ink on parchment. Compass rose in one corner.
