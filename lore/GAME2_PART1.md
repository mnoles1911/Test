# Game Two: The Singing Vault — Acts I & II

---

## Premise

The Vault of Aen-Vael lies below the Hollow Hearth within Khorumzad, the deepest dwarven kingdom. King Bromrin's gold-hunger — deliberately accelerated by the Ashen Hand mole Drossvik — has driven his diggers to breach both chambers. The gold-hunger is now spreading through Khorumzad's atmosphere, contaminating the hold. The Ashen Hand knows the vault seal was disturbed and is moving to exploit it.

The Aeluvain — the fallen Eighth Star, the only thing that can truly end Mordvar — is in the Vault. Roland must reach it before the contamination makes Khorumzad impassable and before Vaeroth's forces seal the approaches.

**Corvus Tane** joins at the beginning of Game Two. The Conclave sent him to report what he has been sensing from the volcano; Roland's arrival interrupts that errand.

---

## Act I: The Siege Approaches

### Between Games

In the months since Game One:
- Roland has sent a message to Coldstoke asking Aldric Vane to go nowhere and speak to no one about his family history (it did not arrive in time — Aldric has already had one attempt on his life)
- Corvus's Conclave assignment to Khorumzad was triggered by his sensing the Vault's disturbance from Eldermark
- The Ashen Hand has moved a significant force to the eastern approach to Khorumzad's entrances
- Bromrin is now largely confined to his throne room; his regent-in-practice is his daughter, Darna Deepdelver

### Opening

Roland arrives at Khorumzad's outer gate to find an Ashen Hand encirclement already beginning — not a siege yet, but a cordon. They are not trying to stop people from entering. They are trying to ensure no one leaves with the Aeluvain.

Inside, Darna Deepdelver provides the situation: Bromrin has moments of clarity decreasing in frequency. Drossvik is still in the court — she does not know he is the source. Three of the nine dig levels below the great hall have been contaminated; workers brought back up are showing early gold-hunger symptoms that resolve slowly in clean air. The tenth level — the Hollow Hearth approach — has been sealed.

**Corvus's first action:** He walks the contaminated levels without a mask, senses the spread, and maps it. He comes back pale. He tells Roland the contamination is deliberate — it has a direction, a source, a propagation pattern that matches designed release rather than accident. Someone in the court has been managing it.

**Dagna's first action:** She goes to the same levels with chalk and a measuring line and records the seismic profile. When she surfaces, she compares her readings with Corvus's map. The contamination propagation and the seismic instability are not co-located. She tells Roland: *"Whatever is spreading from the source is moving west. The deep structural shift is moving east. Two different problems."* The second problem is the Vault — the breach below has changed the stone's behavior in ways that will not be visible until something heavy is placed on the wrong floor. She begins marking load-bearing points throughout the accessible sections. By Game Two's climax, this is what stops the tunnel collapse from becoming a full level cascade.

---

### [MAIN QUEST] Exposing Drossvik

The evidence is in the ceremonial wine stores — Corvus can trace the magical signature of the compound to its point of introduction. Drossvik has been adulterating the ceremonial wine that Bromrin alone consumes at the weekly court ritual.

**The confrontation:** Roland presents the evidence to Darna, not Drossvik directly. Darna's response is methodical — she secures the wine stores, removes Drossvik from court access, and then invites Roland to the meeting where Drossvik is confronted.

Drossvik, cornered, provides information. He tells them: the compound is reversible in theory — the Surgeons' School in Solgrade has been studying gold-hunger physiology with Thrarin's funding, and the research Corvus's environmental magic combined with the School's findings can produce a treatment protocol. He tells them: Vaeroth knows the Vault was breached and has had a secondary agent in the hold besides Drossvik — someone lower, still active. He does not know who.

He is not forgiven. He is used. He considers this fair.

---

### [MAIN QUEST] Bromrin's Treatment

The treatment protocol Drossvik described requires:
1. Corvus's environmental magic to isolate the contamination spread
2. Compounds from the School's research (which Roland sent for after Game One — they arrive by courier midway through this act)
3. Bromrin's cooperation during a period of clarity

The treatment scene is the trilogy's most personal sequence. Bromrin, in a clear moment, asks Roland directly: *Will this work?* Roland says he does not know. Bromrin says: *Good. I prefer honest men to kind ones.*

The treatment does not fully restore Bromrin. It arrests the artificial acceleration and allows natural recovery to begin. He will have more clarity more often. He will not be who he was before Drossvik. He knows this. He proceeds anyway.

**[SIDE QUEST] The Second Agent:** Drossvik indicated there is another agent in the hold. Orion, whose Brotherhood training in infiltration and observation is suited for this, spends two days quietly watching court staff behavior. He identifies a kitchen worker who has been absent from his post at specific intervals that match the contamination's spread patterns. The worker is confronted; he is not an ideologue, only someone who was paid and threatened into it; he cooperates; the contamination stops spreading.

## Act II: The Descent

### [MAIN QUEST] The Nine Levels

The descent to the Vault of Aen-Vael requires passing through nine dig levels below the great hall and the sealed Hollow Hearth approach. Each level presents escalating difficulty:

**Levels 1-3:** Heavy contamination but passable with Corvus's protection. The gold-hunger in the ambient air manifests as a pressure on perception — everything valuable looks more valuable; every decision feels like it should be made quickly; the urgency is artificial and both Corvus and Roland know it, which helps.

**Levels 4-6:** The walls have traces of Second Age construction — this was excavated deliberately long before the dwarves arrived. Someone built these chambers. The architecture is not dwarven. It is not any recognizable style. Seren is quiet for a long time looking at the walls, then says: *This was built before the Third Age. Before the Second. This was here when the world was young.*

Dagna, who has been marking every junction, stops at the keystone of Level Six's primary arch. There is a name carved in it — old Aelorin formal script, the construction used for persons of exceptional historical consequence. She does not know the name. She describes it to Seren.

Seren is still for a moment. Then she says: *"I do not know that name either."* She says nothing more. Roland notices that this is not the same as the name not being in her memory — Seren is four hundred years younger than Aelthurion, but she is a loremaster. An Aelorin name she does not know, in Vigil-Keeper formal script, on a chamber wall two thousand years below the light, is not a name that was forgotten. It is a name that was removed.

**Level 7:** The party encounters Ashfallen — three former Khorumzad soldiers who were captured during an Ashen Hand incursion and returned. They wear familiar faces. Two of Bromrin's personal guard who accompanied the descent freeze on recognition. One does not survive the hesitation.

**Level 8:** The Hollow Hearth approach. The air here is wrong — not contaminated, but old. As if the air has not moved in centuries. Corvus says the magical contamination from above does not reach this level. Something else is present. He cannot name it.

**Level 9 / The Vault Approach:** The seal Bromrin's diggers breached is visible — a circular door of Second Age metal with seven slots in a pattern matching the Sundered Crown. The slots are empty. The door is already open.

**The implication:** Someone else has been here. The Vault was breached not just by Bromrin's gold-hunger obsession but deliberately — someone reached the seal and opened it from the right side. The door opens inward. Whatever was inside came out.

Or: the Aeluvain let itself be found.
