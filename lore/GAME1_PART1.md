# Game One: The Sundered Crown — Acts I & II

---

## Premise

The Sundered Crown — Mordvar's own artifact, the crystallised form of a hollowed human will — was shattered two thousand years ago when the Grand Alliance unmade him at the Sorrowmarsh. Its seven pieces were lost to history. Now the Ashen Hand is quietly recovering them, kingdom by kingdom, to send east to the Ash Tower in the Sundered Isles, where the smith Hadran Volthen waits to reassemble the Crown and complete Mordvar's return.

Roland Ashford knows the iron pommel on the Iron Chalice's founding altar is one of the seven pieces. He has been hunted since he said so. He does not yet know who else is looking for the pieces — or that he is in a race he has only just learned exists.

---

## Act I: The Hunted Scholar

### Opening

Game One opens with Roland being chased through Aldenholt at night. He is not running from the Iron Chalice directly — they expelled him without official charges. He is running from someone hired by someone who wants to know what he found. He does not yet know who.

He reaches the Loremaster's Archive and the scholar Henrietta, who has been quietly compiling genealogical research she did not fully understand. She gives him a duplicate she has prepared — a copy of her notes connecting an iron pommel to Second Age records of the Sundered Crown. She presses it into his coat at the door with an urgency she does not explain.

Within twenty-four hours, Henrietta is dead, her quarters searched, the original notes gone. Roland has only the duplicate.

**This is the first murder. It is what proves to Roland that the pommel matters and that hesitation will kill him.**

---

### [MAIN QUEST] The Pommel — Piece One

The iron pommel is in the Iron Chalice's chapel in Aldenholt. Roland cannot walk in the front door — he is expelled and being watched. Separately, an Ashen Hand agent has been positioned in Aldenholt for three months, waiting for an opportunity to acquire the piece. Roland does not know this yet.

The Order's financial crisis gives him a route. Dame Calla Vane has been secretly meeting with a Solgrade banking agent to negotiate the debt extension. Roland intercepts the agent, learns the terms, and realizes he can offer something the agent cannot: evidence of House Korvath's weapons-smuggling operation, which the banking house has been trying to document because Korvath is undercutting their caravan contracts.

This information earns him a private meeting with Dame Calla. The meeting does not resolve the expulsion — she cannot undo it without reopening the question of the pommel — but she gives him access to the chapel for forty minutes, alone, with the light off.

Roland takes the pommel. He also takes nothing else, replaces the altar fixture with an iron rod of identical weight, and tells Calla what he has done before he leaves. She does not stop him. She does not acknowledge him either. He understands.

The Hand agent learns of the acquisition within a day. He reports east. The race has a name now.

**Piece One acquired.**

---

### [MAIN QUEST] Henrietta's Thread

The duplicate Henrietta gave Roland contains references to five more pieces — the gold coin (Solgrade), the bronze ring (Vosskara), the silver clasp (Aelorin Greatwood), the copper wire (Sailor's Guild Archive, Copper Isles) — and one she had a reference for but could not trace to a location. Her notes are partial because she was killed before she finished.

Her research points to a Second Age genealogical record in the Archive's restricted section. The record is now inaccessible — her death is officially unsolved but the Archive has been locked to outsiders pending investigation.

**[SIDE QUEST] The Archive's Locked Room:** Roland must find the Archive's assistant archivist, Tomlin, who has been placed on temporary suspension while the investigation proceeds. Tomlin has a copy of the restricted section's access key that he has not reported. He is terrified. If Roland can convince him that knowing what is in the restricted section is the only way to find out who killed Henrietta, Tomlin provides access.

The restricted section contains: the complete noble genealogy of Eldermark back to the Second Age. Roland finds the connection Henrietta was building — Mordvar's bloodline runs through a minor lateral branch, to a family that emigrated to the Vosskaran frontier three generations ago and changed their name. The name they changed it to: Vane.

**Not the Iron Chalice's Grandmaster. A different Vane. A blacksmith in a frontier village called Coldstoke. Aldric Vane.**

Roland does not yet know what this means. He logs it and moves on.

## Act II: The Four Kingdoms

### Overview

The second act spans all four human kingdoms as Roland tracks the remaining pieces. The Ashen Hand is also after each piece — in some kingdoms their operation is further along than others. The order is player-determined but the Solgrade arc is recommended last — it connects the information from the other three.

---

### [MAIN QUEST] Solgrade — The Gold Coin

House Korvath has the gold coin and knows what it is. They have been waiting for someone to come asking. The Ashen Hand has been negotiating with them separately for six months.

Ser Aldric Vossant of the Golden Lance approaches Roland first — he has been watching House Korvath for two years trying to document a weapons-smuggling operation and needs evidence Roland can potentially gather from inside the House. He offers Golden Lance cooperation in exchange.

Roland infiltrates Korvath's counting house using an identity the Crimson Ledger sold him (this costs him a favor he will repay in Game Two). He finds the smuggling evidence and the gold coin.

Korvath's negotiation, when it comes, is sophisticated. They know the coin is a piece of the Sundered Crown. They also know Roland needs it. Their price: Roland publicly endorses House Korvath as the broker for military contracts during the coming war. Roland does not accept this. He offers instead: the smuggling evidence he found — which implicates two other Houses on the Council — in exchange for the coin and Korvath's agreement not to interfere with Roland's work going forward, and not to deal with the Hand.

Korvath accepts. They are pragmatists. The Hand's negotiation collapses.

**[OPTIONAL QUEST] The Vossant Verdict:** Roland provides the smuggling evidence to Vossant. Vossant takes it to the Council. Three Houses are implicated; two are censured; one (House Pelarin) is expelled from the Council entirely. Vossant is promoted to Council liaison. His conviction deepens. Golden Lance commits to the final battle.

**Piece Two acquired.**

---

### [MAIN QUEST] Vosskara — The Bronze Ring

The bronze ring is in the possession of Despot Yaromir, who does not know what it is. It was given to his grandmother by a wandering smith who stayed the winter and paid for his lodging with the ring. Yaromir wears it as a thumb ring because it was his grandmother's and she told him it was old.

The Ashen Hand has not located this piece. Their Vosskaran intelligence is focused on the Tribute War dispute, not artifact recovery.

Negotiating with Yaromir is not about the ring — Yaromir would give Roland the ring if asked plainly, because rings are not armies and he has bigger concerns. The negotiation is about whether Vosskara will commit to the alliance when the time comes.

The conversation that earns Vosskaran commitment is the one where Roland asks Yaromir what he wants to be remembered for. Yaromir, who has spent fifteen years demanding tribute no one will acknowledge is owed, answers: *holding the line when no one was watching.* Roland says: *Then let us make sure someone watches it.* He cannot promise anything — he has no authority — but he acknowledges the thing that has never been acknowledged.

Yaromir gives him the ring.

**[SIDE QUEST] The Tribute Papers:** Frost Brotherhood Captain Mira Halsten has intelligence on Ashen Hand movements along the Vosskaran frontier. She will share it if Roland helps her deliver evidence to Yaromir proving that the Ashen Hand has been agitating the Tribute War dispute from inside both Eldermark and Vosskara — deliberately maintaining the conflict. Yaromir reads the evidence. He is very still for a very long time. Then he says: *I see.* The tribute dispute de-escalates. Not resolved — but quiet.

**Piece Three acquired.**
