## World at a Glance

**World:** Mira-Thal — two continents. Mira (civilized west), Thal (wild east).

**Era:** The Long Twilight — the Third Age, the Age of Men. Humanity is ascending. The world's deeper fabric is fraying.

**Protagonist:** Roland Ashford — former Iron Chalice knight, expelled for refusing silence about a relic that turned out to be a piece of the Sundered Crown.

**Antagonist:** Mordvar. A Second Age human king who emptied himself to escape death, became immortal and empty, and was unmade two thousand years ago beneath the volcano Drûn-Khazad. Mordvar is reforming.

**The Trilogy Arc:**
- **Game One — The Sundered Crown:** Reassemble the seven-piece binding artifact to destroy Mordvar's regent and shatter his command structure.
- **Game Two — The Singing Vault:** Descend into the dwarven hold of Khorumzad to recover the Aeluvain — the fallen Eighth Star, the only thing that can truly end Mordvar.
- **Game Three — The Last Star:** Find Aldric Vane, the last of Mordvar's bloodline and the only person who can wield the Aeluvain against him. Fight the Battle of Drûn-Khazad.

## Tone and Themes

Low fantasy. No chosen ones, no prophecies, no divine intervention. The heroes are people doing the hardest available thing because no one else will. The world has beauty and rot in equal measure. Victories are earned and cost something. The enemy is not evil in the abstract — Mordvar was a man who made a terrible choice out of ordinary fear, and understanding this does not make him less dangerous.

The governing theme: **bonds between people are more durable than institutions, and more powerful than any single act of heroism.**
