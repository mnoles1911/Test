CALLA: [authoritative] I was told you had information on House Korvath's freight contracts.

ROLAND: [matter-of-fact] Manifests. Four shipments, two routing falsifications, a name on the receiving end that will interest the Council Banking Committee more than it interests you.

CALLA: [calm] What do you want for it?

ROLAND: [quietly] Forty minutes in this room. Alone. Light off.

CALLA: [serious tone] You understand I can't undo the expulsion. Not without reopening the question formally.

ROLAND: [calm] I know that.

CALLA: And you're not asking me to.

ROLAND: No.

CALLA: [reflective] What you found... what you believe is on that altar... I don't dispute the scholarship. I want you to know that. I never disputed it. The decision I made was about what the Chalice could survive, not whether you were wrong.

ROLAND: [matter-of-fact] I know that too.

CALLA: [quietly] Does it not bother you? That I know you're right about the pommel, and I still... that the decision was--

ROLAND: [tired] It bothered me for a year. Then it stopped bothering me and started feeling like information instead.

CALLA: [softly] Information about what?

ROLAND: [reflective] About how institutions work. About what they protect and what they decide they can't. [pauses] I don't think you're wrong that the Chalice would have collapsed if this had come out three years ago. I think the Chalice collapsing might have been the right outcome and the WRONG price.

CALLA: [quietly] You've gotten blunter.

ROLAND: [light chuckle] Three years of not having an institution to be careful on behalf of. It does that.

CALLA: [calm] The Korvath information. You could have sold this.

ROLAND: I could have.

CALLA: Why didn't you?

ROLAND: [matter-of-fact] Because I need the pommel, not the money. And because the Korvath situation is its own problem that the Council should probably know about. I'm not in the habit of hoarding useful information to sell later. It goes bad. [pauses] Henrietta taught me that.

CALLA: [softly] She was a good woman.

ROLAND: [calm] Yes. She was.

ROLAND: [quietly] Someone killed her because of work she was doing on the same subject. The same artifact, the same lineage records. I want you to know that, not because it changes anything between us, but because I think you should know what you're standing next to.

CALLA: [hesitates] Standing next to.

ROLAND: [serious tone] You didn't kill her. But the secret that made her dangerous is the same secret that made me expendable. I thought you deserved to hear that plainly.

CALLA: [quietly] I should have found another way.

CALLA: [authoritative] Forty minutes. The lamp stays off. Whatever you find, whatever you take... I don't know. You understand what I mean by that.

ROLAND: [quietly] I do.

CALLA: [serious tone] If you find anything beyond the artifact... any record, any document... it stays here.

ROLAND: I'm only here for one thing.

CALLA: [reflective] The Chalice has three knights in Solgrade on contract. When you reach Solgrade... if you reach Solgrade... they're worth knowing. Ask for Ser Brenn at the Goldfield garrison. Tell him I said the endurance doctrine applies. [pauses] He'll know what that means.

ROLAND: [softly] Thank you.

CALLA: [quietly] Don't linger.
