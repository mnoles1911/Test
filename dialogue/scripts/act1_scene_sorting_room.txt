TOMLIN: [nervous] You shouldn't be... the Archive is under restricted access. I thought you knew that. Everyone knows that.

ROLAND: [calm] I know it.

TOMLIN: Then--

ROLAND: [matter-of-fact] The clerk at the front desk has a copy of Fenwick's Territorial Surveys, third edition, on his reading stand. The real third edition, not the corrected reprint. He's been reading it since I came in. He didn't look up. [pauses] He won't look up for another hour, probably.

TOMLIN: [quietly] You shouldn't be here.

ROLAND: [softly] Where are you staying? Since the suspension.

TOMLIN: [stuttering] I... Maret's boarding house, on the Weavers' side. Why would that--

ROLAND: Are you going back tonight?

TOMLIN: [nervous] I don't see what that--

ROLAND: [quietly] Tomlin.

TOMLIN: [hesitates] I haven't decided.

ROLAND: [matter-of-fact] The restricted section. You have a copy of the access key.

TOMLIN: [rushed] I don't know who told you that, but I don't have... that key was reported in with the inventory when--

ROLAND: [calm] You cut a copy three years ago because Henrietta asked you to. In case she was ever incapacitated and a research request couldn't wait. You kept it because getting rid of it felt like getting rid of the reason she gave it to you.

ROLAND: [quietly] I know you haven't reported it because if you had, the investigation would have flagged it by now and you'd be in a different kind of trouble than a suspension.

TOMLIN: [nervous] Whatever you think you know about the restricted section... whatever you found in Henrietta's work... I don't think you understand what you're asking me to risk. My position here is--

ROLAND: [softly] You don't have a position here. You have a suspension and a boarding house room you haven't decided whether to go back to.

ROLAND: I'm not saying that to diminish you. I'm saying it because I think you already know it, and I'd rather not waste your time pretending I don't.

TOMLIN: [quietly] What do you think is in there?

ROLAND: [matter-of-fact] A noble genealogy. Second Age lineage records. Something Henrietta was building toward... a connection she couldn't finish.

TOMLIN: [nervous] The restricted section has any number of records. Noble lineages, monastic charters, deed transfers from the pre-Consolidation period... which is to say, the category you're describing would encompass--

ROLAND: [quietly] She's dead, Tomlin.

ROLAND: [reflective] She made me a copy of her notes two nights ago. She pressed them into my coat at the Archive door. She already knew someone was watching. She didn't run. She made me a copy and she stood at the door until I was gone and then she went back inside.

ROLAND: [tired] I've been thinking about that. [pauses] I think she knew what she was doing. I think she knew what it was going to cost. And I think the reason she made the copy instead of running is that she believed someone needed to finish what she'd started.

ROLAND: [quietly] I'm trying to finish it.

TOMLIN: [softly] She taught me to catalogue.

TOMLIN: [reflective] When I came here I didn't... I had a clerk's certification, which is to say I knew systems, but the things she knew about physical documents, about how to read age and condition and provenance from the material itself... she didn't have to teach me that. That wasn't my job. She taught me anyway because she said anyone working here ought to understand what they were actually handling. [sighs] She had strong opinions about that.

ROLAND: [quietly] Yes. I imagine she did.

TOMLIN: [serious tone] If I give you the key. If. I don't... I want to be clear that I'm not--

TOMLIN: [serious tone] I need you to tell me that what you find in there matters. Not as a promise. I know you can't promise that. I need you to tell me you believe it.

ROLAND: [calm] She's dead. Someone made sure she would be before she finished. Whatever is in that room, they already knew it was there. [pauses] That's enough for me to believe it matters.

TOMLIN: [quietly] The lock is stiff. Lift the handle while you turn it. She showed me that.

ROLAND: [softly] Go back to Maret's tonight.
