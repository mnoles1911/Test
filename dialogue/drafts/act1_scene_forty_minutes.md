# Draft: Forty Minutes

**Scene:** Act I, Aldenholt — Iron Chalice Chapel, after hours
**Characters:** Roland, Dame Calla Vane
**Story function:** Roland trades the Korvath weapons-smuggling evidence for forty minutes alone in the chapel with the lights off. Acquires the iron pommel (Piece One of the Sundered Crown). Requires `korvath_evidence_acquired = true` and `calla_meeting_arranged = true`.
**TTS script:** `scripts/act1_scene_forty_minutes.txt`

---

## Scene-Specific Voice Notes

**Calla:** Eldermark aristocracy, worn in. Has been giving orders for thirty years and her voice knows exactly how much force each sentence requires. Never raised. Warmth is in the register but arrives rarely. The stillness of someone who made her decisions a long time ago and has been living in them since.

**Roland:** Baseline calm, slightly more reflective than usual — this is a conversation he has been preparing for in the back of his mind for three years. The two beats where his voice opens up: the dry "Three years of not having an institution to be careful on behalf of" and the moment he confirms Henrietta died because of the same secret.

**The dynamic:** They both know the expulsion was wrong. Calla knows Roland knows. Roland knows Calla knows. Neither of them re-litigates aloud — that's not the kind of strength either of them has. The whole scene is the texture of two intelligent people speaking around the thing they will not name. Until, briefly, Calla names it: *I should have found another way.* Roland does not rush to fill the silence after. That restraint is the entire emotional payoff.

---

## Setting

Night. The chapel is lit by a single lamp Dame Calla has left burning near the door. The altar is at the far end — stone, clean-swept, the iron pommel mounted at its center. The altar fixture is old. The room is old. It smells of cold stone and candle wax and the specific quietness of places that have been cared for a long time.

Dame Calla stands near the door. She is not waiting — she has been doing something else and stopped when Roland arrived. Her coat is still on. She does not sit.

## Script (Prose)

**DAME CALLA**
I was told you had information on House Korvath's freight contracts.

**ROLAND**
*(produces a folded document — sets it on the nearest pew)*
Manifests. Four shipments, two routing falsifications, a name on the receiving end that will interest the Council Banking Committee more than it interests you.

*(She looks at the document without picking it up.)*

**DAME CALLA**
What do you want for it?

**ROLAND**
Forty minutes in this room. Alone. Light off.

*(She looks at him. Long enough that most people would say something. He doesn't.)*

**DAME CALLA**
You understand I can't undo the expulsion. Not without reopening the question formally.

**ROLAND**
I know that.

**DAME CALLA**
And you're not asking me to.

**ROLAND**
No.

**DAME CALLA**
*(measured)*
What you found — what you believe is on that altar — I don't dispute the scholarship. I want you to know that. I never disputed it. The decision I made was about what the Chalice could survive, not whether you were wrong.

**ROLAND**
*(evenly)*
I know that too.

**DAME CALLA**
*(quietly)*
Does it not bother you? That I know you're right about the pommel, and I still — that the decision was —

**ROLAND**
It bothered me for a year. Then it stopped bothering me and started feeling like information instead.

**DAME CALLA**
*(quietly)*
Information about what?

**ROLAND**
*(after a slight pause)*
About how institutions work. About what they protect and what they decide they can't.

I don't think you're wrong that the Chalice would have collapsed if this had come out three years ago. I think the Chalice collapsing might have been the right outcome and the wrong price.

*(A silence. This is not the kind of thing most people say to Dame Calla Vane.)*

**DAME CALLA**
*(carefully)*
You've gotten blunter.

**ROLAND**
*(a dry note, brief)*
Three years of not having an institution to be careful on behalf of. It does that.

*(She almost smiles. She doesn't, quite.)*

**DAME CALLA**
The Korvath information. You could have sold this.

**ROLAND**
I could have.

**DAME CALLA**
Why didn't you?

**ROLAND**
*(plainly)*
Because I need the pommel, not the money. And because the Korvath situation is its own problem that the Council should probably know about. I'm not in the habit of hoarding useful information to sell later. It goes bad.

Henrietta taught me that.

**DAME CALLA**
*(carefully, quietly)*
She was a good woman.

**ROLAND**
*(evenly)*
Yes. She was.

*(Beat. When he continues, his voice is neutral on purpose.)*

**ROLAND** *(cont.)*
Someone killed her because of work she was doing on the same subject. The same artifact, the same lineage records. I want you to know that, not because it changes anything between us, but because I think you should know what you're standing next to.

*(Dame Calla is very still.)*

**DAME CALLA**
Standing next to.

**ROLAND**
You didn't kill her. But the secret that made her dangerous is the same secret that made me expendable. I thought you deserved to hear that plainly.

*(A long pause. The lamp flickers.)*

**DAME CALLA**
*(very quietly — not to Roland, quite, but not away from him either)*
I should have found another way.

*(Roland says nothing. He does not rush to fill it, does not reassure her, does not accept the partial confession on her behalf. He simply lets it stand.)*

*(After a moment:)*

**DAME CALLA**
*(back to herself — not entirely, but enough)*
Forty minutes. The lamp stays off. Whatever you find, whatever you take — I don't know. You understand what I mean by that.

**ROLAND**
*(quietly)*
I do.

**DAME CALLA**
*(picking up the Korvath document, not looking at him)*
If you find anything beyond the artifact — any record, any document — it stays here.

**ROLAND**
I'm only here for one thing.

*(She nods. She moves toward the door. Pauses.)*

**DAME CALLA**
The Chalice has three knights in Solgrade on contract. When you reach Solgrade — if you reach Solgrade — they're worth knowing. Ask for Ser Brenn at the Goldfield garrison. Tell him I said the endurance doctrine applies. He'll know what that means.

**ROLAND**
*(genuinely — a slight surprise)*
Thank you.

*(She looks at him for one moment longer. Something passes — not quite guilt, not quite grief. The expression of someone who has been carrying a specific weight for three years and has just, unexpectedly, had it acknowledged.)*

**DAME CALLA**
*(quietly, going)*
Don't linger.

*(She leaves. The door does not close fully — she has left it for him to pull shut behind her. Roland stands alone in the lamplight for a moment. He looks at the altar. Then he reaches up and turns the lamp off.)*

---

## Post-Scene Environmental Beat (no dialogue)

After Roland has gone, Dame Calla returns to extinguish the lamp. It is already off. She picks up the iron rod from where Roland left it on the altar's edge — identical weight, identical size. Set there without explanation, without apology. Just the acknowledgment: *I took nothing else. I left you what I could.*

She holds it for a moment. Then she sets it back.

This is a fixed-camera cutscene of 8–10 seconds. No voice line. Worth it for the closure.

## Designer Notes

- The Solgrade knights handoff at the end is a soft setup for Act II — Calla is giving Roland a resource he wouldn't otherwise have. It is also, quietly, an apology she cannot speak. Cast and direct it that way.
- "Standing next to" originally read "adjacent" in the prose. We changed it because (a) it's more spoken, and (b) the TTS pipeline can't tag abstract concepts and the word was carrying its weight on subtext alone. The lesson is general: when stripping to script, prose words that lean on subtext usually need replacing with concrete ones.
- Ten seconds of silence in this scene matters more than any single line. Calla's "I should have found another way" requires the silence after. Do not cut that pacing in a future revision for length.
