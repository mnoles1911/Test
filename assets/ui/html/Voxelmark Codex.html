Aldenholt
— a holdfast on the Vain, between Ashwood and the sea
Population
~ 240 souls
Region
Vainfold
Notable
The Brotherhood chapel
Weather
Light rain · 11°

Aldenholt sits where the Vain bends west toward the sea. The holdfast was raised in the second age by Maelren the Quiet, and its inner walls are older still — said to be built upon stones laid by the Brotherhood when their order was young and the Sundered Crown was whole.

The town survives on the river and on the road that runs east to Hollowford. Most who come stay one night and move on; few have reason to linger in a place where the chapel bell still rings the old hours.

"The bell rings even when no hand is on the rope. Halren says that is how the Brotherhood remembers itself, but I have seen the rope move, and there is no one there." — A miller's daughter, in the long winter

The chapel is the only building still tended by the Brotherhood. Brother Halren lives there alone since the others left for Brothers' End, and the door is kept open at all hours, even in storm.
